# PRAJNA: Commercial Reactor Validation Strategy & Roadmap

## Executive Summary
In nuclear engineering, operating commercial power plant SCADA telemetry is legally classified or restricted due to nuclear security regulations (AERB/BARC in India, US NRC 10 CFR 73.54, IAEA NSS No. 17). No independent entity or startup has direct, unrestricted public internet access to live commercial reactor control room telemetry.

This document establishes the **four scientifically and institutionally legitimate pathways** for advancing PRAJNA from an academic prototype to commercial reactor validation.

## Pathway 1: International Commissioning Benchmarks (Immediate Open Access)

### Where to Find Data
The **International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)** and the **OECD Nuclear Energy Agency (NEA)** maintain de-identified, high-resolution digitized commissioning datasets measured directly on operating commercial nuclear power plants.

1. **IAEA Coordinated Research Projects (CRP) on PHWR/CANDU:**
   - *Example:* IAEA CRP on "Thermal-Hydraulics of Heavy Water Reactors".
   - *Data:* Actual commissioning test measurements recorded during plant startup of commercial 220 MWe and 540 MWe PHWR units (e.g., turbine trip tests, full load rejection tests, primary pump trip / natural circulation cooldown).
2. **OECD/NEA Reactor Physics & Thermal-Hydraulics Database:**
   - *Example:* Kalinin-3 VVER-1000 benchmark, Peach Bottom commercial BWR turbine trip benchmark, TMI-1 MSLB benchmark.
   - *Data:* Full-core instrumentation time-series measured on commercial units during controlled safety transient validation.

### Implementation in PRAJNA
- Ingest published benchmark time-series into `data/raw/commercial_benchmarks/`.
- Validate PRAJNA's $T_{\text{margin}}$ and temperature/pressure predictions against actual physical recordings from these commercial commissioning tests.

## Pathway 2: Regulatory Licensing Codes (RELAP5 / TRACE / CATHENA)

### Why Regulators Accept This
Nuclear safety authorities (AERB, US NRC, CNSC) evaluate digital safety systems against **NQA-1 quality-assured, regulatory-certified engineering codes**. If a system performs accurately against certified licensing codes, it achieves pre-licensing qualification status.

| Code | Governing Authority | Primary Reactor Architecture | Target Physical Phenomena |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RELAP5 / TRACE** | US NRC / INL | LWRs, Advanced Reactors | Best-estimate two-phase thermal-hydraulics, critical heat flux |
| **CATHENA** | Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) / CNL | CANDU / PHWR | Horizontal channel voiding, header pressure dynamics, thermosiphoning |
| **ATLET** | GRS (Germany) | Commercial PWR/BWR | Plant transient and accident thermal-hydraulics |

### Implementation in PRAJNA
- Develop standard input/output couplers for RELAP5 and CATHENA deck outputs.
- Benchmark PRAJNA against 10,000+ Monte Carlo transient runs generated by RELAP5/CATHENA under certified uncertainty parameters.

## Pathway 4: Full-Scope Replica Simulator Access

Commercial nuclear plants maintain **1:1 Full-Scope Replica Simulators** for licensed operator training (e.g., manufactured by WSC / Western Services Corp, CORYS, or GSE Systems).
- These simulators model every instrument channel, valve, and trip circuit in the actual plant to certified real-time fidelity.
- **Advantage:** Unlike the actual plant, the replica simulator can be deliberately subjected to catastrophic accidents (large break LOCA, complete station blackout) to test emergency tripping without risking physical assets.
- Academic institutions with nuclear engineering departments (e.g., Purdue, Texas A&M, IIT Kanpur) frequently have access to plant simulator environments.
